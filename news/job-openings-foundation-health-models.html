Job Openings: Doctoral & Postdoctoral Researchers in Foundation Health Models

Published on July 2, 2026 • Department of Computing, University of Turku & ELLIS Institute Finland

The Department of Computing at the University of Turku and ELLIS Institute Finland invite applications for a full-time Doctoral Researcher position and a full-time Postdoctoral Researcher position. Both roles are integrated into the high-impact research project, FINe-Health Foundry, in collaboration with ELLIS Institute Finland and partner institutions.

Host Institution: University of Turku (Department of Computing, Faculty of Technology)
Affiliations: ELLIS Institute Finland & University of Turku
Location: Espoo or Turku, Finland (On-site)
Application Deadline: August 17, 2026 at 16.00 (Europe/Helsinki)

Position 1: Doctoral Researcher in Foundation Health Models

The Department of Computing at the Faculty of Technology invites applications for a full-time Doctoral Researcher position. This role focuses on developing a nationwide, versatile foundation health model using Finland's national health databases. The fixed-term position starts based on mutual agreement, and ends 30.9.2029 with potential extension.

Research Pillars

The successful candidate will focus on the following pillars:

  • Pretraining Foundation Health Model: Researching and building training pipelines for foundation models trained on nationwide health databases and structured longitudinal electronic health records.
  • Medical Reasoning and Grounding: Developing advanced post-training techniques to ensure foundation health models reason accurately over medical knowledge graphs and clinical instructions while verifying factuality and safety boundaries.
  • Agentic Methods for Virtual Labs: Constructing interactive "virtual laboratories" where medical professionals can interact with, audit, and align LLM-powered medical agents with clinical guidelines.

Requirements

  • MSc degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Machine Learning, or a closely related quantitative field.
  • A solid foundation in Computer Science with a deep understanding of Artificial Intelligence and related algorithms.
  • A strong interest in or prior experience with medical AI, Natural Language Processing, or clinical data analysis.
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Programming skills and experience with deep learning frameworks (PyTorch, Hugging Face, etc.).
  • Good communication skills in English (written and spoken).
  • A strong track record of publications in top-tier AI/NLP conferences will be a plus.

Position 2: Postdoctoral Researcher in Foundation Health Models

The Department of Computing at the Faculty of Technology invites applications for a full-time Postdoctoral Researcher position. This role focuses on leading the development of pretraining pipelines and clinical alignment techniques for clinical foundation models. The fixed-term position starts based on mutual agreement, and is initially for 2 years.

Research Pillars

The successful candidate will lead activities in the following pillars:

  • Pretraining Foundation Health Model: Leading the design and scaling of pretraining pipelines on nationwide health databases and structured longitudinal electronic health records.
  • Medical Reasoning and Grounding: Developing advanced post-training and alignment techniques (RLHF, DPO, GRPO) to ensure foundation health models reason accurately and safely.
  • Agentic Methods for Virtual Labs: Researching and constructing interactive agentic frameworks and virtual labs for expert human-in-the-loop auditing and alignment.

Requirements

  • Ph.D. in Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Computer Science, or a closely related field.
  • A strong track record of publications in top-tier AI/NLP conferences.
  • Advanced experience in training large language models or handling multi-modal clinical data.
  • Strong organizational skills and experience in collaborative research tasks.
  • Good communication skills in English (written and spoken).
